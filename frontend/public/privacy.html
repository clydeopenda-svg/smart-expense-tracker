1. Information this app stores

Smart Expense Tracker stores information that you enter into the application to provide its account, budgeting, and expense tracking features.

  • Your account username
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way hash — the application never stores your actual password
  • Transaction descriptions
  • Transaction amounts
  • Transaction types, such as income
