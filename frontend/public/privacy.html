Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

1. Information this app stores

Smart Expense Tracker stores information that you enter into the application to provide its budgeting and expense tracking features.

  • Transaction descriptions
  • Transaction amounts
  • Transaction types, such as income or expense
  • Transaction categories
  • Transaction dates
  • Your monthly budget amount

2. How your information is used

The information you provide is used to operate the application and display your financial dashboard.

This includes calculating income, expenses, balances, budget progress, category totals, and spending insights.

3. How information is stored

Transaction and budget information is stored by the application's backend so that your dashboard can retrieve and display your data.

You should avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for tracking your finances.

4. Information sharing

The current application is designed to use your transaction and budget information for its core functionality. It does not intentionally sell your financial information or use it for advertising.

5. Your choices

You can add, update, and delete transactions through the application. You can also update your monthly budget whenever needed.

If you need assistance with information stored by a particular deployment of the application, contact the project owner through the contact channel associated with that deployment.

6. Data security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information stored by the application. However, no internet-based application or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application changes or when its data practices change. Any updated version should include a revised effective date.

8. Questions

Questions about this Privacy Policy should be directed to the project owner through the contact information provided with the application's current deployment.

This page describes the current application's intended data practices and is not legal advice.
